Trophy Bingo Basics

What is the story of Trophy Bingo?

Goldie, a charming, ambling Golden Retriever, and her four-pawed pals were all living a wonderful dog’s life when a threat came into their world. Earnest Getmyway, AKA The Trophy Hunter, has set his sights on new quarry – snaring one of every dog breed for his formidable animal collection.

Together with Goldie, find her friends and rescue them from Earnest. Each time you complete the Bingo objectives and win a level, you receive an item that brings you one step closer to rescuing the captured dogs. Collect all the items, free the dogs and band together to save the others.

Your mission:

  • Play Bingo or spin the Wheel to unlock new dogs and Chapters.
  • Gather the collection items for a dog in order to rescue it.
  • Rescue special dogs with Power Play abilities and bring them into gameplay to help you win.
How do I select a level?

Select levels from the Trophy Bingo world map. Click any level you have unlocked to play it.

How do I select my Power Plays?

After you choose a level, and before the Bingo game starts, select the Power Plays (your Dog Team) to use in that level. You can pick any Power Plays you have unlocked.

How do I play Bingo?

Trophy Bingo follows the same rules as standard Bingo.

Each Bingo card has 25 squares, in 5 rows and 5 columns. The centre square is a free space and is already daubed. The columns are labelled B-I-N-G-O, and the squares hold random numbers from 1 to 75. Bingo balls are called in the centre of the screen, each showing a letter and a number (for example G20, I15 or N75). Daub (mark) each square on your card that matches a called ball. Score a BINGO by marking the 4 corners of a card, or a horizontal, vertical or diagonal line of 5 squares.

How do I call Bingo?

Each Bingo card has a Bingo Button at the bottom. When you are sure you have met the requirements, press the button to call Bingo! If you call Bingo before you have met the requirements (a Bad Bingo), Chester the cat appears on your card to tease you. There is no other penalty for a Bad Bingo.

How many cards can I play with?

Before each level, choose to play with 2 or 4 cards.

What are Pattern Bingos?

In levels with a Pattern Bingo, daub the shape of the pattern on your card to claim the Bingo.

What are Bingo Combos?

In levels with Bingo Combo objectives, you must call Bingos that overlap. For example, a Double Combo needs 2 Bingos that cross on the same card.

What is a Mystery Ball?

The Mystery Ball is a special ball that appears during a level. Click it to release bonuses onto your cards. A bonus activates when you daub its square.

What is the Wheel?

Spin the Wheel to rescue more dogs from Earnest Getmyway! The Wheel appears after you complete a level. Spin it to find and unlock dogs. Once a dog is unlocked, spin its own wheel to nab collection items and rescue it. During special events, look out for the Bow-wow Wheel on the event map.

What do Diamonds do?

Use Diamonds to:

  • Buy more Bingo cards to play.
  • Buy Treats.
  • Buy collection items.
  • Spin the Wheel for a chance to win collection items, or find a dog!
  • Buy more Bingos if you run out in a level, so you can keep playing.
  • Buy Lucky Numbers.
How do I get more Diamonds?
  • Buy Diamond packs with iCoins in the Store.
  • Watch a short video in the Store for a free reward.
  • Collect Daily Rewards and complete Daily Challenges.
How are levels organized?

The world map is divided into Chapters of 20 levels each. Travel through the Chapters to rescue every dog.

Troubleshooting

I can’t beat this level. What am I doing wrong?

Make sure you complete all the level’s objectives. Many levels have Bingo Combos or Patterns, and you need to do something special to get the WIN! Check that every number you daubed has been called, so you don’t call a Bad Bingo. Clear any Blockers that stand in the way of a Bingo. See the Blockers section below.

I paid for something and I never got it!

Oh no! Email our support team at support@trophybingo.com. Tell us your iWin username, the date of the purchase, what you bought and how many iCoins it cost. We’ll help you ASAP.

I got a Bad Bingo. What does that mean?

You called Bingo before you met the requirements, so Chester the sneaky cat appears on your card to taunt you. There is no other penalty. Make sure you have daubed all the correct numbers and cleared any Blockers before you call Bingo.

Blockers

What are Blockers?

Chester, Earnest’s trusty sidekick, will do anything to help his master. He throws Blockers onto your Bingo cards to slow you down. Clear a Blocker when its number is called.

What kinds of Blockers are there?

There are 6 Blockers: Sand, Mud, Grass, Ice, Tennis Balls and Discs.

How do I clear Sand, Mud, Grass and Ice?

Daub any square next to the Blocker.

How do I clear Tennis Balls?

Daub the Tennis Ball.

How do I clear Discs?

Discs are the hardest Blocker to clear. Daubing a Disc moves it to another square on your card. Each daub makes the Disc flimsier, and the final daub clears it.

Collections

What are collection items?

Each dog has its own set of collection items. Collect them all to rescue the dog. You must find the dog first to see which items it needs.

What happens when I collect all the items in a collection?

You free the dog! Once a dog is free, use its abilities to help you win.

How do I get collection items?
  • Get 3 stars on a level.
  • Win items by spinning the Wheel.
  • Buy items with Diamonds.

Power Plays and Skills

What are Power Plays?

Trophy Bingo is Bingo with a twist! Alongside the standard Bingo rules, you can use Power Plays to help save Goldie’s friends. Each dog you rescue has a Power Play or a special skill. Choose your Power Play dogs carefully to build the perfect Dog Team before each level.

What are Treats?

Treats activate Power Plays. Fill the Power Play meter, then feed your dog a Treat to use its Power Play.

How do I fill my Power Play meter?

Daub squares on your cards.

Which dogs have Power Plays?

13 dogs have Power Plays. These are the Super Dogs, and they make up the Dog Team you bring into each level.

What kinds of Power Plays are there?

There are 3 kinds: Automatic, Active and Wait.

  • Automatic: activates as soon as you click the Power Play icon.
  • Active: you select a card or square to use it.
  • Wait: marks a square. Daub that square to get the Power Play’s benefit.
What are Helper Dogs?

There are 11 Helper Dogs, each with a special skill to help you on your journey. Rescue them to unlock their skills: extra Dog Team slots, score boosts, a chance to start a level with a free daub or a bonus on your card, and MUCH MORE!

Bonuses

How do I get bonuses?
  • From the Mystery Ball.
  • At the start of a level, if you have rescued a Helper Dog.
  • With a Bonus Bingo purchase.
What is a Spin Bonus?

Daub it to get free spins on the Wheel.

What is a Score Bonus?

Daub it to get 100 extra points!

What is a Power Play Meter Bonus?

Daub it to fill your Power Play meter instantly, so you can use a Power Play right away.

What is a Bomb Bonus?

Some Power Plays place a Bomb on a random square. Daub the Bomb to set off an explosion of daubs: every square around it is daubed.

What is an Instant Bingo Bonus?

Some Power Plays give an Instant Bingo Bonus. Daub it to get 1 Bingo instantly!

Other Players

Who are the players on my map?

When you are signed in to iWin, the world map shows other iWin players who are at levels near yours. Hover over a player’s picture to see their name.

Who is in the column beside my cards?

The column beside your cards shows the players who call Bingo during the game. Hover over a square to see the player’s name.

Daily Rewards

How do I get Daily Rewards?

You get a Daily Reward each day you play Trophy Bingo. Each day in a row that you return, the reward grows. The biggest reward comes on the last day, so come back every day to claim it!

What can I get from Daily Rewards?
  • Diamonds
  • Treats
  • Free spins on the Wheel

Lucky Numbers

What are Lucky Numbers?

Do you have a lucky number? Customize your cards with Lucky Numbers! Once you buy a Lucky Number, it appears on your cards in every level. You choose the number, the card and the square.

You cannot put the same Lucky Number twice on one card (for example, two B1s). Lucky Numbers are not called more often than other numbers.

How do I pick a Lucky Number?

Click the Lucky Numbers icon on the map. Select the card, then the square you want to change, then the number.

Can I remove a Lucky Number?

Yes. Removing a Lucky Number is free, and the square goes back to a random number during play. If you want to put a number on that square again, you pay for it again.

How do I change a Lucky Number?

Buy a new Lucky Number for that square to replace the old one.

Daily Challenges

What are Daily Challenges?

Every day brings a brand-new set of Bingo challenges. Complete all the challenges in the set before the next day to get the reward.

When do I get my prize?

You get the reward when you complete the Daily Challenge. If you forget to claim it, claim it when you log in the next day.

What can I win from Daily Challenges?

Diamonds!
