Privacy Policy

How Greenstone Games looks after your personal information across Trophy Bingo.

Last updated 1 November 2026

This privacy notice explains how Greenstone Business Services Inc., doing business as Greenstone Games, collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you join the Trophy Bingo Members Club, shop at the Trophy Bingo Shop, contact our support team, take part in our campaigns and promotions, or otherwise interact with Trophy Bingo. It also explains your rights and how to contact us.

About this notice. This notice has two parts, which apply together: this Core Privacy Notice, which applies to everyone; and Schedule A (Regional Terms), which contains additional terms for the place where you live. Please read the Core Privacy Notice together with the section of Schedule A that applies to you. Where a term in Schedule A differs from the Core Privacy Notice, the Schedule A term applies for residents of that territory.

Core Privacy Notice

Who is responsible for your data

Greenstone Business Services Inc., doing business as Greenstone Games (“Greenstone Games”, “we”, “us”), is the controller of your personal information.

Where this notice applies

This notice covers trophybingo.com, the Trophy Bingo Members Club, the Trophy Bingo Shop at shop.trophybingo.com, our Trophy Bingo social media pages, our support email, and our campaigns, promotions and prize draws.

Trophy Bingo itself is played on iWin. iWin runs your iWin account and processes your iCoin purchases under iWin’s own privacy policy. When you give your permission, iWin also shares your Trophy Bingo in-game data with us, and we handle that data under this notice. If you contact us for help with the game, we may share your iWin username and the details you give us with iWin so that we can resolve your request.

What we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Members Club: your first name, email address, your consent to hear from us (with the wording you agreed to and when), your country, and how you found us (the page, referring site and campaign link).
  • Registration and connected accounts: your name, email address, username, date of birth or age, gender, and location (city and country). If you sign in with, or connect, an account on another platform, such as Google, Facebook, Discord, Spotify or Steam, we receive your profile details from that platform and, where the platform and your settings allow, your activity there: what you follow, play, save or listen to. Each platform shows you what it will share before you agree. Schedule B sets out what each platform may share.
  • In-game data from iWin, with your permission: your iWin username and player ID; how you play Trophy Bingo, such as the levels and Chapters you reach, your scores, the dogs you rescue, the Power Plays, Treats and Diamonds you use, and when and how often you play; and your in-game purchases, such as the Diamond packs you buy and the iCoins you spend.
  • Shop orders: your name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping addresses, and what you bought. Card payments are handled by our payment provider; we do not receive your full card number.
  • Support and prizes: what you tell us when you email us, including your iWin username and purchase details, and the shipping address you give us if you win a prize.
  • Website and advertising data: information your browser and device send when you visit our sites, such as IP address, browser type and the pages you view, collected through cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel where we use it.

We do not intend to collect special categories of data (such as health, religion, or political opinions).

Why we use it

We use your personal information to run the Members Club and send you Trophy Bingo news, member rewards and offers you have agreed to receive; to process, ship and support your Shop orders; to understand how you play Trophy Bingo and to tailor rewards, offers and content to you; to answer your support requests; to run campaigns, promotions and prize draws and deliver prizes; to understand and analyse our audience; to measure and improve our advertising; and to personalise and improve what we offer. We use it only for these purposes and purposes compatible with them, as described in this notice.

Our data processor

We use Appreciation Engine Inc., which provides the FairShare platform, as our data processor to capture, organise and analyse this information on our behalf and under our instructions. It stores the information on infrastructure in the United States and may access it from Canada and other locations where its authorised personnel and subprocessors operate. When you connect an account, that platform shows you what it will share before you agree, and you can stop sharing at any time from your FairShare privacy settings or on the platform itself.

FairShare membership

Signing up through FairShare may also make you a FairShare member. As a member you can earn rewards for data you choose to share, and you decide whether to share it with other businesses on FairShare. FairShare’s own privacy policy, which FairShare shows you when you sign up, covers your FairShare membership.

Who we share it with

We share your personal information with service providers who help us operate Trophy Bingo, only as needed for the services they provide to us:

  • Appreciation Engine Inc., which provides the FairShare platform, as our data processor, as described above;
  • Shopify, which runs the Trophy Bingo Shop and processes card payments;
  • Printful, which prints and ships Shop orders and prizes;
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our websites and stores Members Club sign-ups;
  • Google, which provides our email;
  • Meta, where we use the Meta Pixel to measure and target our advertising; and
  • iWin, which runs the game, shares your in-game data with us when you give your permission, and helps us resolve support requests.

We may also share your personal information with members of our corporate group. We do not sell your personal information for money. Where we share your information, we do so in accordance with this notice and applicable law.

International transfers

Your personal information is stored and processed on infrastructure located in the United States by our data processor, Appreciation Engine Inc. It may be accessed by Appreciation Engine Inc. from Canada and other locations where its authorised personnel and subprocessors operate in the course of providing the service. Our other service providers may also process it in the United States, Canada and other countries where they operate. Where your information is transferred across borders, we rely on the safeguards described in the regional section that applies to you in Schedule A.

How long we keep it

  • Members Club: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Shop orders: for as long as tax and accounting law requires us to keep business records, generally six years in Canada.
  • Information processed by Appreciation Engine: raw activity data for up to 30 days, historical data for up to 12 months after you stop being active, and anonymised data for a further 12 months.

In each case we may keep information for a longer or shorter period where the law requires or permits it.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw consent, unsubscribe from communications, and ask us to access, correct or delete your information. Every Members Club email includes a link to unsubscribe, and you can stop sharing a connected account at any time from your FairShare privacy settings or on that platform. The specific rights available to you, and how to exercise them, depend on where you live; see your regional section in Schedule A. To make a request, or exercise any right, contact us at privacy@trophybingo.com.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under the age at which consent for the processing of personal information is required in your territory (see Schedule A for the age that applies where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age without the consent of a parent or guardian where that is required. If we learn that we have collected such information without the required consent, we will delete it promptly.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice from time to time, and we will change the date at the top when we do. For any question, or to exercise a right, contact privacy@trophybingo.com.
