How do I get the best performance on an Android phone or tablet?

If Trophy Bingo is lagging or stuttering, give your device a little room to breathe:

  • Close apps you’re not using. Go to Settings > Apps, tap an app you want to stop and choose Force stop.
  • Free up storage by uninstalling apps you no longer need, or by moving photos and videos off your device.
  • Keep Android up to date. Go to Settings > About and check for software updates.

Menus differ from one Android device to the next, so the exact steps on yours may look a little different.

How do I get the best performance on an iPhone or iPad?

Lag, crashes and stutters usually come down to free memory or storage. Try these:

  1. Restart your device, then open Trophy Bingo again.
  2. Close apps running in the background. Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause (or double-press the Home button on older devices), then swipe the apps away.
  3. Check your storage in Settings > General > iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage).
  4. Clear space by removing apps you don’t use. Touch and hold an app, then tap Remove App.

The Basics

What’s the story?

Goldie, a charming golden retriever, and her four-pawed pals were living the good life until Earnest Getmyway, the Trophy Hunter, came to town. His new obsession: snaring one of every dog breed for his collection.

Team up with Goldie to find her friends and set them free. Every level you win earns you an item that brings you one step closer to rescuing a captured dog. Collect the set, free the dog, and band together to save the rest.

Your mission:

  • Play bingo or spin the Wheel to unlock new dogs and Chapters.
  • Gather each dog’s collection items to rescue it.
  • Rescue the special dogs whose Power Plays help you win levels.

Meet every pup in the pack.

How do I pick a level?

Head to the Trophy Bingo world map and tap any level you’ve unlocked.

How do I choose my Power Plays?

After you choose a level, you pick your Dog Team: the Power Play dogs you’ve rescued and want to bring into that game.

How do I play bingo?

Trophy Bingo follows the standard rules of bingo.

Each card has 25 squares in five rows and five columns, headed B-I-N-G-O, with numbers from 1 to 75. The centre square is free and already daubed. Balls are called in the middle of the screen, each with a letter and a number (G20, I15, N75 and so on). Daub every square on your card that matches a called ball.

Score a BINGO by daubing all four corners, or a full line of five across, down or diagonally.

How do I call bingo?

Press the BINGO button at the bottom of the card once you’ve met the requirements. Call too early (a Bad Bingo) and Chester the cat pops up on your card to tease you. That’s the only penalty.

How many cards can I play with?

Two or four cards on a tablet, one or two on a phone.

What are Pattern Bingos?

In Pattern Bingo levels, you daub the pattern’s shape on your card to claim the bingo.

What are Bingo Combos?

Combo objectives ask you to call bingos that overlap. A Double Combo, for example, means two bingos that cross on the same card.

What is the Mystery Ball?

A special ball that turns up during a level. Tap it to release bonuses onto your cards, then daub them to set them off.

What is the Wheel?

Spin the Wheel to snatch more dogs back from Earnest Getmyway. It appears after you win a level, and spinning it can find and unlock specific dogs. Once a dog is unlocked, spin its own wheel to grab the collection items you need to rescue it. Keep an eye out for the special Premium Wheel during limited-time events.

What are diamonds for?

Spend diamonds to:

  • Buy more bingo cards.
  • Buy more Treats.
  • Buy collection items.
  • Spin the Wheel for collection items, or to find a dog.
  • Buy extra bingos when you run out in a level and want to keep playing.
How many Chapters are there?

The adventure runs across six Chapters of 20 levels each. Play them all to see everything Trophy Bingo has to offer.

Troubleshooting

Why can’t I play four cards on my phone?

Phone screens are too small for four, so phones top out at two. When you choose a level you can pick one or two cards on a phone, or two or four on a tablet.

The game keeps crashing on my Android device.

Make sure Trophy Bingo is the only app running. The steps for closing apps and freeing up memory are in Hot Topics.

The game keeps crashing on my iPhone or iPad.

Make sure Trophy Bingo is the only app running. The steps for closing apps and freeing up memory are in Hot Topics.

I can’t beat this level. What am I doing wrong?

Check you’re completing every objective: many levels need a combo or a pattern to win. Make sure you’re not calling Bad Bingos, that every number you’ve daubed has actually been called, and that you’ve cleared any blockers in your way. See Blockers for how.

I paid for something and never got it!

Oh no! Press the Report an Issue button in the game’s Options menu and tell us the date of purchase, what you bought and how much it cost. You can also email support@trophybingo.com. We’ll sort it ASAP.

I got a Bad Bingo. What does that mean?

You pressed BINGO before you’d met the requirements, so sneaky Chester shows up on your card to taunt you. There’s no other penalty. Double-check you’ve daubed the right numbers and cleared any blockers, then go again.

Blockers

What are blockers?

Chester, Earnest’s trusty sidekick, will do anything to help his master. He throws blockers onto your cards to slow you down. Clear a blocker when its number is called.

What kinds of blockers are there?

Six: Sand, Mud, Grass, Ice, Tennis Balls and Discs.

How do I clear Sand, Mud, Grass and Ice?

Daub any square next to it.

How do I clear Tennis Balls?

Daub the tennis ball itself.

How do I clear Discs?

Discs are the toughest. Each daub knocks the disc to another square on your card and makes it a little flimsier. The final daub clears it for good.

Collections

What are collection items?

Every dog has its own set of collection items, and you need the full set to rescue it. Find the dog first to reveal which items it needs.

What happens when I complete a collection?

You free the dog, and its ability joins your team to help you win.

How do I get collection items?
  • Earn three stars on a level.
  • Win them on the Wheel.
  • Ask your friends, and receive them as gifts.

Power Plays and Skills

What are Power Plays?

Trophy Bingo is bingo with a twist. Every dog you rescue brings a Power Play or a special skill to help you win. Build your perfect Dog Team before each level.

What are Treats?

Treats fire your Power Plays. Fill the Power Play meter, then feed your dog a Treat to unleash its Power Play.

How do I fill the Power Play meter?

Daub squares. Every successful daub fills it up.

Which dogs have Power Plays?

Thirteen Super Dogs have Power Plays, and they make up the Dog Team you take into each level. See who does what in the pack.

What kinds of Power Plays are there?

Three: Automatic, Active and Wait.

What is an Automatic Power Play?

It fires the moment you tap the Power Play icon.

What is an Active Power Play?

You choose a card or a square to use it on.

What is a Wait Power Play?

It marks a square. Daub that square to collect the Power Play’s reward.

What are Helper Dogs?

Eleven Helper Dogs each bring a special skill to help you along. Rescue them to unlock their skills.

What skills do Helper Dogs unlock?

Extra slots for your Dog Team, all kinds of score boosts, the chance to start a level with a free daub or bonus on your card, and plenty more.

Bonuses

How do I get bonuses?

Mostly from the Mystery Ball. Rescued Helper Dogs (the dogs without Power Plays) can also give you a bonus at the start of a round, and a Bonus Bingo purchase comes with extras.

What is a Spin Bonus?

Daub it for free spins on the Wheel.

What is a Score Bonus?

Daub it for 100 extra points.

What is a Power Play Meter Bonus?

Daub it to fill your Power Play meter instantly, ready to use.

What is a Bomb Bonus?

Bombs come from Power Plays, not the Mystery Ball. A bomb lands on a random square, and daubing it sets off an explosion that daubs every square around it.

What is an Instant Bingo Bonus?

Also a Power Play bonus rather than a Mystery Ball one. Daub it and you get a bingo on the spot.

Playing with Friends

What is the first-time login bonus?

What is the Mailbox?

Where gifts, requests and challenges from other players land. A badge shows how many new messages are waiting. From the Mailbox you can:

  • Receive a gift: accept it and send a gift of Treats back, or delete and ignore it.
  • Receive a request for help: send help to your friend, or delete and ignore it.
How do leaderboards work?

Every level has its own leaderboard, so you can see how your score stacks up against your friends.

What is bragging?

Beat a friend’s score on a level and you can brag about it. Send it off and see if they can top you.

How do I send and receive collection items?

Help your friends finish their collections faster by gifting items. Tap a dog on your My Dogs screen to see which items you can gift, then tap an item to send it. You need more than one of an item to gift it.

You can also request items for your own collections from the Collections Wheel screen or the My Dogs screen: pick the item and choose Request. You’ll need to be logged in to Facebook with friends on your list.

How do I invite friends to play?

Use the Invite button on the Social screen or on the leaderboards before and after a game to bring up your Facebook friends. You can invite each friend once. You’ll need to be connected to Facebook.

How do I see how many dogs my friends have rescued?

Tap a friend’s profile picture on the main map, or use the Social button, to see the dogs they’ve rescued.

What’s on my friend’s progression screen?
  • How many dogs they’ve rescued.

How do I follow my friends’ progress?

Once you’re logged in, friends who play Trophy Bingo with Facebook show up on the map, with their profile picture next to the level they’ve reached.

Lucky Numbers

What are Lucky Numbers?

Got a number that brings you luck? Buy it as a Lucky Number and it appears on your cards in every level. You choose the number, the card and the square.

You can’t put the same Lucky Number twice on one card (no two B1s), and Lucky Numbers are no more likely to be called than any other number.

How do I pick a Lucky Number?

Tap the Lucky Number icon on the map. Choose the card, then the square, then the number you want.

Can I remove a Lucky Number?

Yes, for free. The square goes back to a random number during play. Putting a Lucky Number back on that square costs more diamonds.

How do I change a Lucky Number?

Buy a new Lucky Number to replace the one on that square.
